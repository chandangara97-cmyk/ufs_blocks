How this price is built: Vehicle cost uses the cheapest valid combination of Sedan (4 seats, ₹3,200/day off-peak · ₹5,300 peak), SUV/Innova (7 seats, ₹5,200 · ₹6,600) or Tempo Traveller (14 seats, ₹5,600 · ₹8,800) for your group size, plus ₹50/night driver halt per vehicle and a flat ₹350 toll & parking charge per vehicle. Hotel is priced at ₹1,480 / ₹2,020 / ₹3,360 per person per night for Budget / Premium / Luxury (+15% in peak season: May, June, December, January). A 10% service fee and 25% operating margin are added on top. All prices are per person for the full trip, rounded to the nearest ₹50, and exclude flights/trains, entry tickets, meals unless stated, permits, personal expenses and travel insurance — see each package page for full inclusions.
