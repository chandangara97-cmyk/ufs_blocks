Boundary Generator

Visualize boundaries and export secure data packages.

Procedure:

1. Filter by Town and IV Unit to locate your area.
2. Upload KML ZIP to overlay boundaries on the map.
3. Click 'Export Bundle' & enter password (123) for CSV + KML download.

Block Finder

Real-time GPS identification for on-field surveyors.

Procedure:

1. Enable GPS/Location permissions on your mobile device.
2. Wait for your blue location marker to appear on the map.
3. View the top panel for the Block ID you are currently standing in.
Open Tool

KML Converter

Convert raw coordinate text into map-ready files.

Procedure:

1. Paste raw Multipolygon/WKT data into the text area.
2. Assign names/labels to each geometry if required.
3. Click 'Generate KML' to download files for Google Earth.
