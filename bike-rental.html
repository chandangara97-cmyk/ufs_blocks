Spiti Valley Motorcycle Tour — 6 Nights / 7 Days

The ultimate Himalayan motorcycle adventure. Shimla to Manali via Chitkul, Kalpa, Kaza, Chandratal Lake. Convoy ride with backup vehicle & expert mechanic.

1,000+ km

Difficulty
Day 1
Shimla → Sangla (230 km)

Ride from Shimla through Narkanda and Rampur along scenic valleys to Sangla. Evening at riverside camp or traditional homestay.

Day 2
Sangla → Kalpa via Chitkul (100 km)

Ride to Chitkul — India's last inhabited village near the Indo-Tibet border. Return and push to Kalpa for jaw-dropping Kinner Kailash views.

Day 3
Kalpa → Tabo (160 km)

Follow the Sutlej River through Nako, stop at the 500-year-old Gue Mummy Monastery, and arrive at Tabo's legendary 1,000-year-old monastery complex.

Day 4
Tabo → Kaza via Dhankar (50 km)

Detour to the cliff-hanging Dhankar Monastery before checking into Kaza — the commercial heart of Spiti Valley. Acclimatize and rest.

Day 5
Kaza Local Exploration

Cross the Chicham Bridge, tour Key Monastery, post from Hikkim (world's highest post office) and see the Langza Buddha Statue.

Day 6
Kaza → Chandratal Lake (100 km)

Conquer Kunzum Pass and descend to Chandratal — the Moon Lake. Camp under a brilliant Himalayan sky in deluxe tents.

Day 7
Chandratal → Manali (120 km)

Ride through Batal and Gramphoo parallel to the Chandra River, arriving in Manali by afternoon — completing the legendary 1,000 km loop.

Kinnaur Valley Loop — 5 Nights / 6 Days

Shimla to Chitkul and back — orchard roads, Kinner Kailash views, ancient temples and the greenest valleys in Himachal. A hidden gem route for serious riders.

~500 km
Total Distance
Shimla Loop
Route
Easy–Mod
Difficulty
Day 1
Shimla → Rampur (130 km)

Leave Shimla and ride through Narkanda's deodar forests, descend to Rampur — the trade gateway to Kinnaur. Overnight at a budget guesthouse.

Day 2
Rampur → Sangla via Karcham (100 km)

Follow the Baspa River into Sangla Valley — arguably the most beautiful valley in Himachal. Stop at Karcham Wangtoo Dam. Overnight at Sangla.

Day 3
Sangla → Chitkul & Kalpa (60 km)

Morning visit to Chitkul — the last village before the Tibet border. Afternoon at Kalpa for majestic Kinner Kailash panoramas. Apple orchards everywhere.

Day 4
Kalpa → Sarahan → Narkanda (170 km)

Descend from Kinnaur, stop at Sarahan's famous Bhimakali Temple — a masterpiece of Himachali architecture. Overnight at Narkanda's apple belt.

Day 5
Narkanda → Hatu Peak → Shimla (65 km)

Morning ride to Hatu Peak (3,400m) — one of the highest motorable points near Shimla. Panoramic views of snow peaks before returning to Shimla.

Day 6
Buffer / Departure

Optional rest day in Shimla. Local sightseeing on rented scooty or departure as per schedule.

Common Questions

Rental FAQs

What documents do I need to rent a bike or scooty?+
A valid government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar Card or Passport) and a valid driving licence are required. For motorbikes, a motorcycle (gear) licence is necessary. Scooties only need a standard licence (MCWOG).
Can I get the bike delivered to my hotel in Shimla?+
Yes — Rent Bull Bike offers delivery within Shimla city. WhatsApp +91 70181 38847 with your hotel address and rental details for quick confirmation.
Are helmets provided with the rental?+
Yes, helmets are provided with all rentals. Additional helmet for pillion available on request.
What is the fuel policy?+
For local day rentals, fuel is not included — bikes are provided with a full tank and must be returned with a full tank. For guided tour packages (Spiti, Manali etc.), fuel is fully included.
Can I ride to Kufri or Chail on a scooty?+
Yes — both Kufri and Chail are accessible on a scooty (Activa/Jupiter). Roads are paved and manageable for moderate riders. For Chail, a slightly more powerful scooty or a bike is recommended due to longer climbs.
Is there a security deposit?+
A refundable security deposit is applicable. Amount varies by vehicle type. Contact Rent Bull Bike directly at +91 70181 38847 for current deposit amounts and terms.
