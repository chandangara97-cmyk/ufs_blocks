Our Story

Luxotic India was born from a deep love for the mountains, people and unique culture of Himachal Pradesh — and a conviction that exploring it doesn't have to mean choosing between authenticity and comfort. We plan every itinerary the way we'd plan our own: real routes, vetted stays, and honest advice, delivered with the polish of a boutique travel house.

What started as a small collection of district guides has grown into a complete Himalayan trip-planning platform — every district mapped, curated stays and package tiers, an interactive route builder, live pricing, and a growing encyclopedia of local culture, food and festivals, all built from time actually spent on these roads.

Our Mission

To make world-class Himachal travel simple to plan and worth remembering — supporting the local guides, homestays and drivers who make it possible, and giving every traveller, from first-timers to seasoned road-trippers, information they can actually trust.
